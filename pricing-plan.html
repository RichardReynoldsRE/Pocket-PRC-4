Vendor Billing Model

Solo / Team / Large Team

  • Pocket PRC controls the 3 vendor ad spots
  • Pocket PRC invoices vendors directly via Stripe
  • Revenue goes to Pocket PRC

White Label — Default

  • Client manages which vendors appear in their app
  • Client collects from vendors on their own (outside platform)
  • Pocket PRC charges setup ($500) and change ($250) fees

White Label — Automated Vendor Billing Add-On (Stripe Connect)

  • Client onboards to Stripe Connect as a connected account
  • Client sets their own vendor pricing
  • Vendors pay through the platform automatically
  • Pocket PRC takes a 5–10% platform fee on all vendor payments
  • Client gets a dashboard showing vendor payment status
  • Fully automated recurring billing — no manual invoicing
